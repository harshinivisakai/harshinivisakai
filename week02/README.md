# WEEK 2 — PROBLEM IDENTIFICATION, LOGIC & APP CREATION

## Day 1 – Problem Identification & 5S

### Understanding Problem Identification & 5S

We started with **Problem Identification**, where we learned to observe problems and think about possible solutions. We were also introduced to the Japanese **5S methodology**:

- **Seiri** – Sort
- **Seiton** – Set in Order
- **Seiso** – Shine
- **Seiketsu** – Standardize
- **Shitsuke** – Sustain

### Hands-on Application: Organizing the Red Cubical

With the help of the 5S method, we organized and managed the tools in the **Red Cubical**, which helped us understand how proper organization can improve efficiency and productivity.

> **Day 1 takeaway:**  
> Proper organization is not just about cleanliness; it is the operational foundation for efficiency, safety, and clear thinking.

## Day 2 – Algorithms & Think Like a Coder

### The Podi Dosa Algorithm

On Day 2, we learned how to create an algorithm for making **Podi Dosa**. It was a simple activity, but it helped us understand how a process can be broken down into clear, step-by-step instructions.

```
Heat Tawa → Pour Batter → Circular Spread → Drizzle Ghee → Sprinkle Podi → Flip & Toast → Serve Crisp
```

### "Think Like a Coder" Series

We also watched the **“Think Like a Coder”** series and completed all **11 episodes**. The videos introduced us to concepts such as:

- **Problem-solving & Logic**
- **Algorithms**
- **Loops & Iteration**
- **Conditions & Branching**
- **Computational Thinking** through interesting stories and challenges.

> **Day 2 takeaway:**  
> Every complex human task is an algorithm in disguise. When you break it down into clear steps, anything can be solved.

## Day 4 – MIT App Inventor: College Connect

### Mobile App Development

On Day 4, we worked with **MIT App Inventor** and created an app called **College Connect**.

The purpose of the app was to help students easily know about college activities, events and updates happening in different departments.

This activity gave us practical exposure to app development and showed us how technology can be used to solve everyday problems faced by students.

> **Day 4 takeaway:**  
> The best applications are born out of empathy for everyday student challenges.
